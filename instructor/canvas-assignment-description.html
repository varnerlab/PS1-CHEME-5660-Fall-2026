Problem Set 1: Treasury Valuation and Funding Risk

Initial submission deadline: Sunday, September 20, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

Download and instructions

On the release page, scroll to Assets and select Source code (zip). Extract the archive before beginning. This assignment was built and tested with Julia 1.12.7.

Choose your track

Enter standard or advanced in TRACK.txt, then complete the matching files:

  • Standard: src/Standard.jl and responses/Standard.md.
  • Advanced: src/Advanced.jl and responses/Advanced.md.

Advanced includes the Standard valuation work and adds a funding comparison. An accepted Advanced score of 4 earns one Magic Point, awarded once for PS1.

Complete your code and all three discussion answers. Keep the function docstrings and answer markers. Leave the supplied support code, tests, data, reports, and checker unchanged.

Check your work

From the extracted folder containing Project.toml, run this setup command once:

julia --project=. --startup-file=no -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()'

Setup needs an internet connection. Then check your saved work with:

julia --project=. --startup-file=no check_submission.jl

The checker runs the tests, displays the results for your discussion answers, and creates MANIFEST.txt. Tests are expected to fail until you complete the starter code. The checker does not upload anything to Canvas.

Submit to Canvas

  1. Zip the entire problem-set folder after running the checker.
  2. Rename the archive to CHEME-5660-PS1-<your netid>.zip.
  3. Replace the complete <your netid> placeholder, including the angle brackets, with your actual NetID. For example: CHEME-5660-PS1-abc123.zip.
  4. Upload the ZIP file to this Canvas assignment.

Submit a readable ZIP containing attempted work by the initial deadline, even if incomplete or tests fail. If you cannot run the checker, submit without MANIFEST.txt. Missing, empty, or unreadable submissions receive a locked score of 0 and are ineligible for revisions or Magic Points.

Revisions

After the initial deadline, eligible students may revise and resubmit PS1 until December 19, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET. Return to this same Canvas assignment, select New Attempt, and upload the revised ZIP file.

Canvas may label these revisions “late.” That is expected and does not create a penalty under the infinite-revision policy. Each revision will be graded, and the highest score earned will be retained.

You may switch tracks before submitting or on an eligible revision. Update TRACK.txt, complete that track's code and answers, and rerun the checker. Only the selected track is graded; Advanced work does not automatically receive a Standard grade. We keep your highest score across tracks.

Collaboration and academic integrity

Submit independent work. You may discuss the problem set with classmates, but you may not share code or solutions directly. You may use Julia documentation, AI tools, and internet resources, but you remain responsible for understanding and explaining everything you submit.

The reference solution will be released after the initial deadline. You may consult it to understand mistakes and debug your work, but your revision must remain your own implementation. Copying the reference solution results in a locked score of 0 for this assignment. These rules also apply to revisions.
